# VeraFi — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script 🎙️

A timed walkthrough to pitch **VeraFi** to judges and evaluators.

---

### [0:00 - 0:45] The Hook & The Problem
> *"Judges, meet Ramesh Ji. He has run a tea stall and kirana store for 8 years, serving over 150 customers every single day. His business is profitable, yet when he applied for a small ₹20,000 loan to buy a commercial refrigerator, the bank rejected him.*
>
> *Why? Because like 1.4 billion unbanked people, all his accounts are recorded in this paper notebook: a Bahi-Khata. To the formal financial system, Ramesh Ji is 'credit invisible'. His only alternative is loan sharks charging 10% interest per month.*
>
> *We built **VeraFi** to fix this."*

---

### [0:45 - 1:30] Live Demo — Shopkeeper Upload & AI Extraction
> *"With VeraFi, Ramesh Ji takes a photo of his handwritten notebook using his smartphone or WhatsApp.*
>
> *(Click: 'Kirana Store Sample' or upload image)*
>
> *In seconds, our multimodal AI Vision pipeline scans the messy handwritten entries. Notice how it categorizes each line into **Jama (money received)** and **Udhar (credit given)**, cross-verifying running balances and filtering out noise.*
>
> *What was once ink on paper is now audit-ready, structured financial intelligence."*

---

### [1:30 - 2:15] The VeraScore™ & Fair Microloan Eligibility
> *"Next, our credit scoring engine analyzes 4 pillars: cash flow velocity, business consistency, customer diversity, and collection cadence.*
>
> *VeraFi calculates a **VeraScore of 745 (Grade A)**, unlocking an instant pre-approved microloan of ₹35,000 at just 1.2% per month—less than one-sixth of what informal lenders charge.*
>
> *With one click, Ramesh Ji submits his loan application directly to partner microfinance institutions."*

---

### [2:15 - 2:45] The Institutional Lender View
> *(Toggle: 'Lender / MFI Portal')*
>
> *"On the lender side, microfinance officers see a real-time risk queue. Instead of sending field agents to physically inspect paper books, the lender views the verified ledger snapshot side-by-side with AI metrics and approves the loan in a single click.*
>
> *This slashes underwriting costs by 80% while expanding lending to underserved communities."*

---

### [2:45 - 3:00] Conclusion & Impact
> *"VeraFi turns informal paper into formal financial reputation. Thank you, and we'd love to answer any questions!"*
