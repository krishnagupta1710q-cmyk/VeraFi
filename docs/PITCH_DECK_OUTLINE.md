# VeraFi — Pitch Deck Outline 📊

Recommended 7-slide pitch deck structure for hackathon presentations:

---

### Slide 1: Cover & Vision
- **Title**: VeraFi
- **Subtitle**: AI-Powered Microfinance for the Credit-Invisible
- **Tagline**: Turning paper ledgers into verified financial reputation.
- **Team**: Krishna Gupta & Janamjai (CSE Undergraduates)

---

### Slide 2: The Problem (Credit Invisibility)
- 1.4B unbanked adults globally; millions of Indian MSMEs rely solely on paper *Bahi-Khata*.
- Zero credit bureau footprint (CIBIL/Experian) $\rightarrow$ Formal banks reject them.
- Predatory loan sharks charge 5%–10% monthly interest (up to 120% APR).

---

### Slide 3: The Solution (VeraFi)
- Three-pillar AI bridge:
  1. **Multimodal OCR**: Instant digitization of handwritten ledger photos via Gemini 1.5 Flash.
  2. **VeraScore™**: Alternative credit risk assessment built on cash velocity and consistency.
  3. **MFI Portal**: Digital underwriting dashboard for low-cost, collateral-free microloans.

---

### Slide 4: Product Demo (Screenshots / Walkthrough)
- [Screenshot]: Upload of physical Bahi-Khata page.
- [Screenshot]: Dual-entry digitized table (Jama vs Udhar).
- [Screenshot]: VeraScore card (745 / 850) with ₹35,000 pre-approved microloan.
- [Screenshot]: Lender loan approval action.

---

### Slide 5: Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend**: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS (Responsive PWA).
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python) + SQLite.
- **AI Engine**: Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision for multimodal document parsing.
- **Scoring**: Custom weighted cash flow & consistency algorithm.

---

### Slide 6: Business Model & Social Impact
- **Monetization**:
  - Origination fee from MFI partners per disbursed loan (0.5% - 1.5%).
  - SaaS subscription for microfinance institutions to replace manual field auditing.
- **Social Impact**: UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 1: No Poverty, SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth).

---

### Slide 7: Future Scope & Conclusion
- WhatsApp & regional voice chatbot for low-literacy users.
- UPI AutoPay integration for daily micro-repayments.
- Final call to action: *"Empowering every honest shopkeeper with the credit they deserve."*
